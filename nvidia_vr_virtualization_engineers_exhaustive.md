##### Histórico de Carreira
Turner Whitted began his career designing digital test equipment, antenna measurement systems, and components of a sonar signal processor. In December 1983, he co-founded Numerical Design Limited (NDL) with Dr. Robert Whitton, serving as president and technical director until 1996, and continued as a director until NDL merged with Emergent Game Technologies in 2005.
